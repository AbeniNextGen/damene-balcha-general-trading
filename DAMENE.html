Company Overview

Damene Balcha General Trading is a multi-sector Ethiopian commercial enterprise committed to quality, operational compliance, and long-term business partnerships.

Headquartered in Wolaita Sodo,South Ethiopia the company manages four distinct activities: trading construction and finishing materials, running Dojamo Guest House, selling two and three-wheeled transport vehicles, and acting as official representative for Phoenix Home Materials.

"Our objective is to deliver value across trading, hospitality, and corporate representations while maintaining absolute legal clarity and customer trust."
